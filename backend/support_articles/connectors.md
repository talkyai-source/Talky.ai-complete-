Connectors link Talk-Lee to external services.

The connectors you can see depend on your account and the current product configuration. CRM connectors such as HubSpot and Salesforce are listed below; call logging is on the Business plan.

![The connectors page, showing connected and unconnected services](/docs/connectors-01-list.webp)

## Which connectors exist

Limited means the connector works only as described here.

| Connector | Status | What it does | What you must set up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Google Calendar** | Limited | Lets the assistant read your upcoming events and book, change or cancel meetings. | Connect Google Calendar under Connectors. Used by the assistant in the dashboard, not by calls. |
| **Gmail** | Limited | Lets the assistant read your inbox and send emails from your account. A call can send a follow-up email. | Connect Gmail under Connectors. A call sends email only when the campaign has an email action and the caller confirms the address. |
| **HubSpot** | Available | Logs calls on the matching HubSpot contact, and creates the contact when an email is known. | Connect HubSpot under Connectors. Call logging is on the Business plan. |
| **Google Drive** | Limited | Lets the assistant list your files and read text files. | Connect Google Drive under Connectors. Used by the assistant in the dashboard, not by calls. |
| **Salesforce** | Limited | Logs calls as Salesforce Tasks, creates Leads for new callees, and lets Salesforce request a call on an outgoing campaign. | Its card appears under Connectors once Salesforce is switched on for the service. Call logging is on the Business plan. |

Each connector is its own connection: connecting one does not connect the others.

## Connect a provider

1. Select **Connectors**.
2. Locate the provider you want to connect.
3. Select **Connect** or **Reconnect**.
4. Complete the provider's authorization process.
5. Return to Talk-Lee.
6. Confirm the connector shows **Connected**.

## Reconnect an expired connector

If a connector shows **Expired** or **Connection needs attention**:

1. Select **Reconnect**.
2. Sign in to the external provider.
3. Approve the required permissions.
4. Return to Talk-Lee and verify the status.

## Disconnect a connector

Use **Disconnect** when you intentionally want Talk-Lee to stop using that connector.
