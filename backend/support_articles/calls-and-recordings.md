## Call History

Call History provides a detailed record of campaign calls.

### How to review calls

1. Select **Call History**.
2. Use search to find calls by phone number.
3. Use available filters such as all calls, lead-related calls, or calls with issues.
4. Expand a call to view additional information.

![Call history, showing the search and filter controls above a list of calls](/docs/calls-01-history.webp)

Depending on the call, Talk-Lee can provide:

- Campaign information
- Call duration/status
- AI summary
- Lead outcome
- Transcript
- Recording playback

### Transcript and Summary

Use the transcript to understand what was actually said. Use the AI summary for a faster overview, but verify important sales or compliance details against the transcript when necessary.

![A call expanded to show its summary and transcript](/docs/calls-02-expanded.webp)

### Call detail page

Select a call to open its own page, where available, for the full record of that single call.

The page has a **Notes and follow-up** card. Select **Edit** on it to change the lead type, the notes, the follow-up form, and the email address or phone number the caller gave. A correction you make to the email or number is kept as your own edit, so a later guess by the agent cannot replace it. Emptying the field removes the detail. The date, the numbers, the duration and the result are the record of the call and cannot be changed.

**Download this call (PDF)** saves one file with everything about the call: its details, what was captured, the AI summary, your notes and the full transcript.

Editing needs a role that may add call details. A read-only role can view and download.

### Table view

Above the list, switch from **List** to **Table** to see the calls as a spreadsheet. White cells can be edited in place: lead type, notes, the three follow-up fields, and the email and number the caller gave. Select a cell and press Enter to edit it. Enter saves and moves down, Tab saves and moves to the next editable cell, and Esc restores the old value. Grey cells are the record of the call and cannot be changed.

### Download the call history

Select **Download** above the list and choose **Excel (.xlsx)** or **PDF (.pdf)**.

- The file holds every call that matches your current search and filters, across all pages, not only the calls on screen.
- Each call comes with its date and time, direction, campaign, numbers, result, duration, lead type, AI verdict, the email and number the caller gave, the best time to call, the AI summary, your notes and the follow-up form.
- The Excel file has a second sheet with totals by result, by lead type and by campaign.
- Dates use your account's time zone, which the file names at the top.

The button stays unavailable for a moment while Talk-Lee finishes loading every call, so a file never misses calls. Transcripts are not part of the list file. Download a single call from its own page to get its transcript.

### Dial again

Where the action is available, use it to place a follow-up call to the same number.

### Call issues

Where a call had a technical problem, Talk-Lee can show an issues panel explaining what happened and what to check.

## Recordings

The Recordings page provides access to stored voice recordings according to your plan and retention policy.

![The recordings page, listing stored call audio](/docs/calls-03-recordings.webp)

Your recording policy is under **Settings → Recording**. **Keep for (days)** there is the retention period: 90 days unless you change it. In this release recordings are not deleted automatically, so download anything you need to keep. The pricing page lists the storage allowance for each plan.

### Recommended usage

Use recordings for:

- QA
- Agent coaching
- Objection analysis
- Conversation-flow improvement
- Troubleshooting audio issues
- Client review where appropriate

Do not share recordings outside authorized users or purposes.

## Analytics

Analytics helps you understand campaign and calling performance.

![The analytics page, showing call trends and campaign performance](/docs/calls-04-analytics.webp)

### Typical metrics

Depending on available data, you can review metrics such as:

- Total calls
- Answered calls
- Failed calls
- Campaign performance
- Call trends over time
- Outcomes

### Recommended workflow

1. Select **Analytics**.
2. Choose the relevant period.
3. Review overall call trends.
4. Compare campaign performance.
5. Use Call History to investigate unusual outcomes.
