## Support availability

In-app support is not enabled by default. This help page does not confirm that a support assistant, ticketing or support email is enabled for your account.

Contact your account owner or use the public contact form to ask for assistance. Wait for confirmation through the available contact channel before assuming that a request has reached the team. Reading this page does not create a support ticket.

Include a short description of the problem, the approximate time and a campaign or call reference when available. Do not include passwords, API keys, recordings or payment details. Ask the account owner for an appropriate secure channel if sensitive information is needed.

The account owner must confirm availability and provide current instructions before you rely on an in-app support workflow.
