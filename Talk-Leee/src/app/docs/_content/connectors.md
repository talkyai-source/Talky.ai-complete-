Connectors link Talk-Lee to external services.

Supported integrations depend on your account and current product configuration. CRM connectors such as HubSpot and Salesforce are available on the Business plan.

## Connect a provider

1. Select **Connectors**.
2. Locate the provider you want to connect.
3. Select **Connect** or **Reconnect**.
4. Complete the provider's authorization process.
5. Return to Talk-Lee.
6. Confirm the connector shows **Connected**.

## Reconnect an expired connector

If a connector shows **Expired** or **Connection needs attention**:

1. Select **Reconnect**.
2. Sign in to the external provider.
3. Approve the required permissions.
4. Return to Talk-Lee and verify the status.

## Disconnect a connector

Use **Disconnect** when you intentionally want Talk-Lee to stop using that integration.
