## Executive summary

Talk-Lee AI is the all-in-one platform to automate and scale phone calls with intelligent voice agents. You configure AI voice agents for inbound enquiries and outbound campaigns using your own approved business information, choose a supported model and voice, set calling rules, and review the effective configuration before any campaign starts.

We work with sales, support and operations teams across healthcare, recruitment, marketing, financial services, travel, retail, real estate, professional services, software and education. The result is more calls answered and made, leads qualified faster, appointments booked automatically, and every outcome recorded for your team to review.

## The challenges we solve

- **Missed inbound calls become lost leads.** Your team cannot always answer every call, especially after hours or when volumes spike.
- **Manual dialing is expensive.** Reps spend their day on rings, voicemails and logging outcomes instead of closing.
- **Follow-up slips.** Warm prospects go cold while waiting for a callback.
- **Qualification is inconsistent.** Each rep screens leads differently, and the pipeline view lags by days.
- **Support queues keep growing.** Repetitive questions consume agent time and produce inconsistent answers.
- **Evenings and weekends go uncovered.** Customers and candidates expect a response whenever they reach out.

## Our services

### AI Voice Agent

An intelligent voice agent that listens, understands, responds and takes action in real time. It answers inbound calls, qualifies leads, books appointments, follows up with prospects and handles customer enquiries around the clock.

What's included:

- Natural-language understanding with intent recognition, context and follow-up questions
- Answers drawn from your business knowledge, services, FAQs, policies and terminology
- Lead qualification, appointment scheduling and information collection
- Outbound workflows such as lead follow-ups, reminders, reactivation and surveys
- Recording of caller requests for your team to review

The outcome for you: **routine calls become completed tasks**, while your team handles the complex problems, sensitive situations and high-value opportunities. Live transfer to a human agent is not currently available; the agent records a request for your team to review.

From your first conversation to a fully operational AI voice agent.

1. **Discover.** We learn how your business works, who your customers are, which calls you handle and what you want to automate.

3. **Train.** We equip your AI with your business knowledge, services, FAQs, policies, terminology and workflows.
4. **Integrate.** We connect your AI agent with the tools and systems it needs to take action and capture information.
5. **Launch.** We test the experience, refine the workflows and put your AI voice agent into action.
6. **Optimise.** We review how the agent performs and continuously refine the experience as your business evolves.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is Talk-Lee AI?**
Talk-Lee AI provides voice agents for configured inbound and outbound calling workflows, with campaign settings and call records.

**Do I need technical expertise to use Talk-Lee AI?**
No. Our guided AI Builder makes it simple to design call flows and prompts without coding.

**Can Talk-Lee AI integrate with my existing phone system or CRM?**
Yes. Talk-Lee AI connects with phone systems, CRMs such as HubSpot, and lead forms.

**How does Talk-Lee AI learn my company information?**
You can upload PDFs and images, or crawl entire websites, to train your agent with the right knowledge.

**Will the AI sound robotic to my customers?**
No. The AI handles a natural, back-and-forth conversation rather than reading a script word for word.

**Can calls be transferred to a human agent?**
Live transfer to a human agent is not currently available. The agent can collect a request for your team to review; this does not arrange a callback.

**Is Talk-Lee AI secure and compliant?**
Access, consent and recording controls are available. Review the configuration, data handling and applicable requirements for your intended use before launch.

**Do you provide phone numbers for campaigns?**
Yes. Talk-Lee AI issues dedicated numbers for your inbound and outbound campaigns.
