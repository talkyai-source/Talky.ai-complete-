# Book a Demo questions: scope decision, 14 November 2026

The user explicitly asked for this work during the production readiness feature freeze: the Book a Demo page listed three questions as text and gave no place to answer them.

Approved scope:
- A form on `/book-a-demo` with fields for the visitor's name and email address and the three questions (business name, services offered, what Talk-Lee AI could improve).
- The answers are saved through the existing public contact-enquiry intake and read by staff on the Admin "Contact enquiries" page. No backend change.
- The Google Calendar booking page opens after the answers are saved, and opens anyway if they cannot be saved.

Intake owner and retention are unchanged from the 4 November record (`2026-11-04-book-a-demo.md`).

This record does not close any work package and does not lift the feature freeze. Other unrelated scope remains frozen.
