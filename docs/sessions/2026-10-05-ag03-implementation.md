## Causes and repairs

1. **An explicit correction expired after 12 later messages.** Realtime checked customer claims against the short contact/readback buffer. Both engines now use the existing immutable live state with a typed unknown/denied/affirmed relationship and caller-turn provenance. Ordinary questions do not erase it; a genuine later caller assertion can reverse it. The short contact buffer remains bounded and serves its existing purpose.
2. **Quoted or unrelated statements could become caller facts.** The shared caller-assertion filter now has a conservative direct-assertion option for relationship facts. A campaign audience, assistant statement, script quotation, reported/conditional/negated statement, another supplier's customer, customer-support occupation or being a merchant cannot manufacture this relationship. The default call-control assertion contract is unchanged. Typed unknown is authoritative, so a retracted assertion cannot be silently recovered from old history.
3. **ASR completion order was mistaken for speech order.** Native speech-start/commit item identity establishes caller ordering independently of final-transcript arrival. A delayed first final may refine relationship evidence if no newer explicit assertion superseded it, but cannot replay contact capture, actions or hangup ownership. Exact duplicates and old revisions do not become new current turns. This uses at most 64 item metadata records, one prior relationship snapshot and the existing 12-message contact buffer. Session-relative audio offsets reject replayed retired anchors when that metadata exists. [Official event-order guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/realtime-transcription).
4. **Traditional media sequence numbers were not unique caller turns.** Flux can suppress a speech-start callback and reuse the media sequence for distinct accepted utterances. The final handler now stamps accepted turns separately; queue drain and false-barge replay retain that order. Exact running/queued duplicates do not advance it. A superseded old task cannot overwrite newer relationship evidence or reissue an older accepted turn.
5. **The depth-one queue could discard a correction.** An ordinary queued question could replace a queued denial before it reached the reducer. Queue replacement now carries only its latest explicit relationship enum/order/ID as bounded predecessor evidence. It does not preserve another transcript or replay the discarded turn's contact, refusal, action or general business state. Current-transcript retraction restores that predecessor where appropriate.
6. **A corrected final could leave stale action confirmation valid.** Native current ASR revisions now advance a separate action-admission token, without pretending they are another caller utterance. The connected-action executor freezes the token before asynchronous policy reads and checks it again at the external-effect boundary. Actual paused-context and paused-claim tests reproduce the previous stale email effect and now prevent it. Existing durable claims and receipts remain intact.
7. **Audio validation could become stale before or during playback.** A delayed denial could arrive after a response passed validation. The native bridge now cancels contradicted unfinished audio and checks the owned utterance again at submission/receipt boundaries. Cancelled gateway continuations and late receipts cannot restore contact or action confirmation. Already completed historical speech remains historical. A normal continuation uses the updated state under the existing one-repair budget; partially submitted audio is never described as wholly withheld or unheard.
8. **Current ASR revisions lacked a retained source record.** The matching original transcript row now retains one bounded latest revision, full-content digest, character count and truncation/retraction flags. Its original content, time, other metadata and row identity are preserved. Exact ownership and sealed-call checks reject a mismatched or late annotation. This preserves evidence; canonical transcript and Lead consumption remains AG05 work.

Provider-final explicit opt-out remains monotonic even if an old item's metadata has been evicted. Such old text cannot gain current hangup/action ownership. Traditional and native prompts remain separate. No governed persona template, model choice or sampling default changed; the dynamic live-state block adds the relationship position and its fully populated fixture remains 741 characters within the existing 768-character limit.

## Remaining acceptance and limits

- **AG04:** qualify actual selected model/voice profiles and controlled calls. The known Groq fabricated-prior-question output remains a semantic failure. A deterministic guard repair does not prove every relationship paraphrase or model claim is recognized. The language parser is deliberately conservative.
- **AG05:** canonical JSON/plaintext, saved summaries and Leads do not yet consume the new revision metadata. Existing plaintext still contains the original ASR. Validate contact withdrawal/correction against the actual confirmation evidence; retaining independently established contacts during this package is not proof that every revised contact remains valid. An initial empty ASR final has no original transcript row under the current accumulation contract. These are explicit handoffs, not completed persistence behavior.
- Metadata-poor native events rely on the existing ordered, non-replayed socket connection. There is no unbounded replay ledger or transparent reconnect/resume guarantee. Traditional callers that bypass the normal accepted-final scheduler retain a compatibility identity without proof of cross-task temporal order. Queue coalescing still answers only the latest queued words; it carries only the latest explicit relationship predecessor.
- State is call-lifetime memory, not restart-persistent storage. Drain incompatible active sessions during coordinated release. This change cannot undo audio already submitted or prove that someone heard it; interrupted/unknown delivery evidence remains truthful.
- The AG02 synthetic retrieval quality gate still fails its unchanged targets. No final release gate passed, no historical Lead was rewritten and the feature freeze remains active. CP05, CP06 and CP09 stay deferred.

## Rollback

No schema rollback is needed. Preserve transcript originals/revision metadata and durable action receipts. Drain active sessions before changing this runtime state contract. Forward-fix or revert to a specifically verified implementation if a regression appears; do not restore known correction loss or stale action admission while labeling the profile approved. Provider/prompt rollback must preserve a verified complete profile and its evidence, not merely an older template hash.
