# Selected outbound warmup lifecycle — local verification

Source candidate: `ccdbabf10150a28543be22d44ffe0e97121cb7ca`, based on `a07d9163f7da732897579bb66529fab5328cc02f`. This is a bounded AG01/AG07 and OP05 repair. It does not complete paid-readiness, live-call acceptance, recovery, or the feature freeze.

The actual supported Asterisk path could discard the selected session while the phone was still ringing. With the existing `DIALER_RING_TIMEOUT_S=300` setting, the endpoint stored the campaign session before ARI origination, but the 180-second warmup sweep removed it even when the provider reported the channel live. The subsequent answer callback constructed process defaults. In the synthetic replay, the selected native/ash tenant session became tenantless cascaded/marin. The ordinary default 30-second ringing window is not claimed to trigger this timing bug.

The unchanged [before/after probe](artifacts/selected-warmup-lifecycle/lost-warmup-probe.py) exercises the real internal `make_call`, Asterisk `originate_call`, local state backend, one watchdog iteration and answer selection through local registration. It records the configured 300 seconds actually submitted to the synthetic ARI port. Ring ages 10 and 181 are simulated by changing only the local monotonic timestamp. DB/guard/ARI/provider/lease ports and the prepared session are synthetic; sockets and DNS are blocked. It stops before pipeline/media execution. [Before](artifacts/selected-warmup-lifecycle/lost-warmup-before.json) and [after](artifacts/selected-warmup-lifecycle/lost-warmup-after.json) retain the exact observations.

The repair uses existing ownership:

- The watchdog snapshots the warmup tuple and creation time before awaits. It releases that exact owner only after current provider absence is known. Live, unknown and failed presence probes retain the selection; a failed new probe cannot reuse an older empty result.
- The answer callback captures existing outbound first-speaker/Answer evidence before awaits. Lost owned selection fails through the existing initialization-error and terminal-outcome path; it does not rebuild current/default configuration. Duplicate callbacks are rejected before pod-capacity checks and again after awaited boundaries, preserving the registered call and its lease.
- A read-only `AsteriskAdapter.has_terminal_event` exposes existing terminal dispatch ownership. Checks before/after status, lease acquisition and ringing wait prevent delayed callbacks from recreating terminal sessions. A lease acquired after terminal cleanup is released through the existing serialized release function; terminal outcomes and retries are not replayed.
- A late ringing callback cannot conceal a lost selection with defaults. Status and factory awaits are followed by ownership checks. A factory result that lost its event reservation or encountered a selected/terminal owner is closed without publishing. Even failed cleanup cannot remove a replacement session. Unowned legacy preparation and subsequent reuse remain covered.

## Remaining boundaries

Unknown provider presence may retain resources until provider/operator reconciliation. This change deliberately does not claim complete orphan recovery, durable session reconstruction, unlimited stale-event retention, or seamless restart. Terminal evidence and warmups remain existing process-local state. No actual provider, PG, PSTN, acoustic/hearing or durable settlement verification occurred in this task. Failed cleanup is recorded, not represented as successful resource release.

Separate read-only probes of the explicitly enabled legacy Twilio/Vonage callbacks are preserved as [script](artifacts/selected-warmup-lifecycle/legacy-cloud-route-probe.py) and [observations](artifacts/selected-warmup-lifecycle/legacy-cloud-route-before.json). The real mounted handlers and token verification can reach defaults when route resolution is unavailable or unresolved. However, their `/answer` routes are outbound-only: signed `To` is the callee, not an authoritative tenant-owned DID. The current durable dialer goes through `/sip/telephony/call` with a planned Asterisk identity; no app caller of the cloud tenant-provider factory was found in the bounded trace. Therefore these separately enabled callbacks remain unqualified for outbound tenant/profile ownership. This repair does not wire cloud campaign origination, choose a tenant from `From`/`To`, or claim that a strict DID-helper flag alone fixes that separate contract.
